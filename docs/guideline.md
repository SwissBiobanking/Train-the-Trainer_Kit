# Step-by-step guideline for new evaluators

This is the **workflow** you will learn during the training and follow in every compliance review.

!!! tip "Before you start"
    Read [Common mistakes in compliance reviews](https://swissbiobanking-my.sharepoint.com/:w:/g/personal/valeria_dicola_swissbiobanking_ch/IQBX0EVtldnXSrFvl8Sj2H7yAb83IvNOIAsJT_tt11tFUKs?e=prsTV8){ target="_blank" } — it shows the pitfalls to avoid at each step.

## Step 1 — Prepare the compliance review

1. Review the biobank's application.
2. Verify that the biobank is eligible for the chosen label.
3. Study the documentation provided (e.g. SOPs, quality manual, sample flow diagrams) and review the biobank's answers to the SQAN online questionnaire.
4. Prepare the interview questions.
5. Finalise the compliance review agenda and send it to the biobank.

## Step 2 — Conduct the compliance review (on site)

=== "Opening meeting"

    Introduce the scope of the compliance review, the roles, confidentiality and the methodology.

=== "Facility inspection"

    - Sample reception area
    - Processing labs
    - Freezers, tanks and backup systems
    - Temperature monitoring and alarms
    - Inventory systems

=== "Document & record review"

    - SOPs
    - Deviations / CAPA
    - Training records
    - Equipment maintenance logs
    - Data protection measures

=== "Staff interviews"

    - Biobank director
    - Quality manager
    - Technical staff
    - Data manager

## Step 3 — Evaluate and score

Once the evidence is collected:

1. Score each criterion in the SQAN.
2. Identify missing requirements and gaps, and write the related recommendations as comments.
3. Propose minimal corrective actions.
4. Draft the preliminary report.

## Step 4 — Communicate the findings

Send an email to the biobank that:

- presents the findings
- highlights the strengths
- lists any missing requirements, the recommendations and the next steps
- explains the timelines

## Step 5 — Write the final compliance review report

- Compile the final evidence.
- Complete the scorecard.
- Review the non-conformities against the evidence.
- Give the final minor recommendations.

## Step 6 — Decision & issuance

- Finalise the compliance review report.
- Give the final decision: **Approved** or **Pending**.
- If approved, issue the label with its validity and the next evaluation date (every 2 years).
