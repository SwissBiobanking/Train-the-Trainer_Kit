# Learning objectives

By the end of the training, participants will be able to:

## Knowledge

1. Explain the purpose, principles and value of the SBP NORMA Label and the SBP OPTIMA Label.
2. Describe the requirements for biobank operations, including sample collection, processing, storage, transport, IT, risk management and quality management systems.
3. Apply the **criteria and indicators** used for the SBP NORMA Label and the SBP OPTIMA Label.

!!! note "What a compliance review checks"
    A compliance review verifies whether the processes of the assessed biobank meet the requirements of the label. These requirements are documented in the SBP quality management manual, through procedures and forms.

## Skills

1. Conduct a full biobank assessment using the standardised compliance review tools.
2. Evaluate compliance based on the biobank's answers to the online questionnaire (SQAN), its documentation, staff interviews and on-site observations.
3. Write clear, structured compliance review reports with evidence-based findings.
4. Give constructive feedback and recommendations to biobanks, including relevant support documents.

## Attitudes

1. Work with objectivity, confidentiality, professionalism and friendliness, and empower biobanks to improve.
2. Work consistently within the defined guidelines and ethical boundaries.
3. Communicate clearly with biobank staff and stakeholders.
