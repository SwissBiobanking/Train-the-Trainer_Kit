# Module 3 — Compliance review techniques & tools

*Methods and tools for conducting a NORMA or OPTIMA compliance review (friendly audit)*

!!! abstract "Objective"
    Gather **sufficient, relevant and reliable evidence** to assess whether the biobank's practices meet the applicable label requirements.

## Topics

- How to prepare for a compliance review
- Document review methods
- On-site evaluation protocol, using the official checklists
- Interview techniques for biobank staff
- Evidence collection and documentation traceability
- Recommendations (major, minor) versus binding requirements

## 1. Compliance review methods — how do we assess?

| Method | How to find evidence |
|---|---|
| **Document review** | Review SOPs, procedures, records, quality documents, QC results, BIMS documentation, etc. |
| **Interviews & discussions** | Ask personnel to explain how processes are performed and documented in practice, and clarify differences between documented procedures and daily activities. |
| **Observations** | Observe processes as they happen, e.g. sample reception, storage, temperature monitoring, quality control or sample retrieval. |
| **Traceability / follow the process** | Select a sample, record or activity and follow it through the different process steps to verify consistency and traceability. |
| **Sampling** | Examine a representative selection of records, samples, equipment or activities rather than reviewing everything. |
| **Cross-checking** | Compare information from different sources: SQAN answers → SOP → records → interview → observation. |

## 2. Compliance review tools — what can we use?

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Checklists** | Guide the assessment and ensure all binding label requirements are adequately addressed. |
| **Compliance review plan / timetable** | Define scope, objectives, processes, people involved, timing and methods. |
| **Notes & evidence records** | Capture and document objective evidence, observations, examples and points requiring clarification. |
| **Process mapping / flow** | Understand how samples, information and responsibilities are managed in the biobank. |
| **Questioning prompts** | Use open questions to explore how processes work and how effectiveness is demonstrated. |

## 3. Choose the appropriate approach

| | | |
|---|:---:|---|
| **Interactive**<br>interviews, discussions, demonstrations | ↔ | **Non-interactive**<br>document and record review, data analysis |
| **On-site**<br>observation of premises and processes | ↔ | **Remote**<br>interviews, document review and virtual demonstrations, where appropriate |

Select the approach according to the **objectives, risks, complexity of the process, availability of evidence and practical feasibility**.

!!! question "Useful questioning prompts"
    - "Can you show me how this is done?"
    - "What happens if…?"
    - "Can you show me the corresponding record?"
    - "How do you know that this process is working?"

## 4. Golden rule: follow the evidence

<div class="evidence-steps">
  <span>Ask</span><span>Observe</span><span>Verify</span><span>Cross-check</span><span>Record</span>
</div>

Draw conclusions from **objective evidence, not assumptions**. Where possible, verify what is described in the biobankSQAN answers and the procedures against what is actually implemented in daily practice.

!!! tip "The spirit of the label compliance review"
    Focus on **understanding the biobank's processes** and **identifying opportunities for improvement**, while objectively assessing the essential label requirements.
