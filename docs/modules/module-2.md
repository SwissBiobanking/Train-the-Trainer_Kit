# Module 2 — SQAN training

The **biobankSQAN** is the online platform developed by the Swiss Biobanking Platform (SBP) where biobanks register, describe their organisation and practices, and receive a quality score. It is the **starting point of every SBP Label**: as an evaluator, you will work with a biobank's SQAN answers and score before every compliance review.

## Purpose and scope of the biobankSQAN

The biobankSQAN, at [www.biobanksqan.ch](https://www.biobanksqan.ch){ target="_blank" }, has three uses:

FIND
:   A public directory to search for a biobank, a sample type, or a biobank infrastructure and its services, with filters.

REGISTER
:   Register a new biobank or biobank infrastructure by completing a questionnaire of about **250 questions**.

VIEW
:   Open the profile of a biobank or infrastructure that is already registered, to update it and see its quality score.

The quality score helps biobanks strengthen their practices by identifying areas for improvement. Each score comes with an explanation of the requirements.

## Eligibility: biobank or infrastructure

Any type of biobank can register and evaluate its organisation in the biobankSQAN. Users register their structure either as a **biobank** or as a **biobank infrastructure**, and this affects what is evaluated:

| | Biobank | Biobank infrastructure |
|---|---|---|
| **Governance evaluation** (legal and ethical, VITA) | Yes, for human biobanks for research regulated by the Human Research Act (HRA) | No |
| **Quality evaluation** (process, resource and quality assurance management) | Yes | Yes |
| **Dashboard** (activity indicators) | Yes (under development) | No |

!!! note "Biobanks not regulated by the HRA"
    The governance score (VITA Label) is designed for human biobanks for research regulated by the HRA, so it does not appear for other biobanks, e.g. diagnostic or animal biobanks. Their specific requirements are still explained in the *Quality indicators* tab.

**Membership:** the full evaluation is available to SBP members. Without membership, a biobank only has access to the governance evaluation.

## Requirements and documentation

**What the answers must describe:** answers must reflect **current practices that are already implemented**, not practices planned for the future. Once new practices are in place, the biobank updates its answers. SBP recommends updating at least **once a year** and sends a reminder.

**Mandatory fields:** few fields are required. They are marked with a **red star** and framed in red. Biobanks don't have to answer every question, but **to apply for a label they must at least answer all the questions marked with that label's symbol**:

| Symbol | Questions used for |
|---|---|
| **R** | Generating the biobank regulation |
| **V** | Legal and ethical scoring (governance), corresponding to the **SBP VITA Label** |
| **N** | Process management scoring, corresponding to the **SBP NORMA Label** |
| **O** | Quality assurance management scoring, corresponding to the **SBP OPTIMA Label** |

**Reference documents behind the criteria:**

=== "Governance (ethical and legal)"

    - Human Research Act (HRA), 30 September 2011
    - Human Research Ordinance (HRO), 20 September 2013
    - Declaration of Taipei (WMA), October 2016
    - Council of Europe Recommendation CM/Rec(2016)6 on research on biological materials of human origin, 16 May 2016

=== "Quality (Good Biobanking Practices)"

    - OECD guidelines for biological resource centres, 2007
    - IARC guidelines for biobanks, 2017
    - ISBER best practices for repositories, 2018
    - NF S 96-900 standard for the management of biological resource centres, 2011
    - ISO 9001 standard for quality management systems, 2015

## The biobankSQAN tool and questionnaire

**Access:** users register, then log in, in the upper right corner of [www.biobanksqan.ch](https://www.biobanksqan.ch){ target="_blank" }. Use a current browser such as Chrome, Firefox or Safari.

**Answering:**

- About **250 questions**; pilot tests showed it takes **less than two hours** if all the information is at hand.
- Questions can be answered in **one or several sessions**, in any order. Click **Save** (upper right) before leaving a page and wait for the green confirmation message: calculating the score can take a few seconds.
- Answer in **English**, so the directory and summaries are uniform across Switzerland.
- To change answers later: open **View my biobank(s)**, select the biobank, and click **Edit**.

**User roles:** the first person to register a biobank or infrastructure automatically becomes its **administrator**. The administrator manages access and can invite:

- **Editors** (read and write)
- **Guests** (read only)

The administrator role can be handed over to someone else in the *Admin* tab.

**Tabs in a biobank profile:**

Summary
:   The characteristics of the biobank or infrastructure. **Public.**

Dashboard
:   Activity indicators (biobanks only, under development).

Quality indicators
:   The biobank's score against SBP's quality criteria.

Admin
:   Access management. Visible to the administrator only.

**What is public and what is confidential:**

- **Public:** the directory and the summary: contact details, organisation (type, purpose, QMS…), associated data (inclusion criteria, pre-analytical data, sample types and numbers) and sample access policy. By registering, the biobank agrees to appear in the directory.
- **Confidential:** the full questionnaire, the dashboard and the quality indicators (scores). The evaluation is never made public.

## Scoring rules

The biobankSQAN assesses **47 mandatory requirements** in four categories:

Governance management
:   Compliance with the law and respect of ethical considerations: management of regulatory affairs, strong governance, a transparent informed consent form, an ethical biobank regulation, a harmonised Material Transfer Agreement (MTA), and staff training.

Process management and Resource management
:   Conformity of IT infrastructure, equipment monitoring and premises control; good management of processes from consent administration to sample shipment, quality controls and validation of methods.

Quality assurance management
:   Effectiveness of the quality management system: documentation management, non-conformity management, risk management, and appropriate preventive and corrective actions.

**Following progress:** an audit trail records profile updates and changes to the answers, so improvements can be followed over time, globally or per category, as graphs in the *Quality indicators* tab.

!!! tip "For evaluators"
    - Before a compliance review, check that the biobank has answered **all questions marked with the symbol of the label** it applies for.
    - Remember that the answers should describe **current practice**: the document review and the on-site visit are where you verify this.
    - Use the score per category to decide where to focus your interview questions and observations.
